## 1. Learn the fundamentals first

Networking basics (IP addressing, DNS, routing), an operating system (Windows or Linux), and the basics of security. Everything else builds on these.

## 2. Pick one direction to go deep

IT is broad: support, networking, cloud, security, project and operations leadership. Try a little of each, then go deep in one. Depth is what gets you hired.

## 3. Choose certifications with a purpose

A certification should open a specific door. Entry-level options such as CompTIA or Cisco CCNA prove your basics. Later, specialised or expert certifications show depth. Don't collect certificates for their own sake.

## 4. Build proof

A home lab, a small project, or a write-up of a problem you solved counts as experience. Publish it on your blog.
